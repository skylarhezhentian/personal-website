E Fund Management · Hong Kong · Dec 2025 – Feb 2026

Deviation salience trading strategy

Do industry-relative salience signals predict next-month momentum or reversal in Chinese A-shares?

Groundwork

Replicated the Carhart four-factor and Fama–French five-factor models on A-shares, adapting factor construction, portfolio formation and regressions to the market's structure and regulatory environment.

Method

  • A reproducible Python pipeline over Chinese equities from 2021 to 2025.
  • Industry-relative salience signals and double-sorted portfolios under equal and value weighting.
  • Seven portfolio specifications, with Fama–MacBeth regressions controlling for size, momentum and volatility.
  • Newey–West inference and missing-return sensitivity tests to check the return patterns across weighting schemes.

Columbia Zuckerman Institute · Jun 2026 – Present

Neural dynamics and behavior prediction

Can compressed recordings keep the brain's fast dynamics, and do global brain states change what individual regions compute?

Compression

Benchmarked local field potential representations from lfpack and autoencoder models against raw recordings. They preserved neural dynamics at high temporal resolution with a 95% reduction in spatial dimension.

Modeling

Modeled simultaneous recordings across brain regions with shared and region-specific latent components, including hidden Markov models and switching linear dynamical systems, to study global and local dynamics across spatial scales.

Now

Comparing continuous, discrete and hierarchical brain-state models to characterize dimensionality and timescales, and testing whether global states modulate regional inputs, local computations, or both. The goal is a dynamic brain atlas.

Laidlaw Foundation · New York · May – Aug 2026

Equitable food routing in New York City

If delivery routes prioritize need, how much more high-need demand do they reach, and what does that cost in total reach?

Model

A Python and Google OR-Tools vehicle-routing model over 528 NYC food pantries and community kitchens, with OSRM travel times, vehicle capacities and service windows.

Experiment

Five allocation policies that incorporate food insecurity rates, solved five times each, with skip-penalty perturbations to test sensitivity.

Result

Need-prioritized routing raised median coverage of high-need demand from 26.3% to 57.7%, with only a marginal decrease in total reach.

Columbia Alpha Partners · Sep 2025 – Present

A structural model of copper prices

What happens to copper when supply is disrupted while AI data centers, electric vehicles and grid build-out all add demand?

Approach

Led an investment pod's copper research, translating geopolitical supply disruptions and these new demand sources into a structural pricing model, then estimating it with 500 Monte Carlo simulations.
